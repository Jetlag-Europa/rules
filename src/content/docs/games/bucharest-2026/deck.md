## Removed From Base Game

- Curse of the Egg Partner
- Curse of the Endless Tumble
- Curse of the Lemon Phylactery
- 5-minute bonus x6

## Added From Expansion Pack Volume 1

- 5% time bonus x4
- 10% time bonus x4
- 60-minute time bonus x1 (note: there are four in the deck, but we are only adding one)
- Time trap x5
- Discard me x2
- Nothing x5
- Discard 3, draw 4 x4
- Draw 2, expand 2 x2
- All curses, except:
- Curse of the Oracle
- Curse of the Runner
- Curse of the Okaihau Express
- Curse of the Seventh Seal
- Curse of the Strongman
- Curse of the Post Office

* = If we can have at least one expansion pack per team, the expansion pack gets added, otherwise, we’re only playing with the home game
