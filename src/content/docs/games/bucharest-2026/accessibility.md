The following modifications will be in effect:
You must decide on a destination before the end of your hiding time and inform Helpdesk of your choice. Once you have informed Helpdesk, you may not change your destination.
If your vehicle is delayed en route and you are no longer able to reach your destination within your hiding time, you must inform Helpdesk and still stick to your chosen destination.
You may walk quickly, but you may not run or jog.
You may not use steps. A step is any surface that rises by at least 10 cm without a gradual slope.
Hiding spots must be accessible – without steps, steep inclines or other obstacles.
You may not board buses or trains if they lack available space for a wheelchair.
Whilst hiding, if you are unable to board the first vehicle out of your first station due to lack of wheelchair space, you may delay your recorded hiding start time (and end time) by the difference between that vehicle and the next vehicle that stops at your pre-chosen destination.
If you do this, you must inform Helpdesk and the seekers – so it’s up to you if you wish to take the extra time or pick another option!

## Deck Modifications for Accessible Mode
🌍 Curse of the Curious Explorer: If the location is not step-free, consider it closed for game purposes.

✋ Curse of the Non-dominant Hand: Both hands may be used to enable movement or other non-gameplay functions.

👸🏻 Curse of the Passenger Princess: If there are only two seekers, someone with limited mobility may not be selected as the driver.

🦈 Curse of the Shark: For any players in a wheelchair, any form of motion counts as walking.

🚶 Curse of the Strider: Remember that you may not run or jog.
