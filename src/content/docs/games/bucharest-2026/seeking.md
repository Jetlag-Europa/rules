You may not use Google Street View, reverse image search, OpenStreetMap queries or any form of artificial intelligence (AI) when seeking.

## Tentacle Questions

- Museums within 2 km
- Libraries within 2 km
- Movie theatres within 2 km
- Hospitals within 2 km
- Covrigarie* shops within 2 km
- * = Not in original Home Game, special to H+S Bucharest 2026

## Feature Definitions

For matching and measuring questions, only features located within the game area count. The source of authority for locations is Google Maps, and measuring should be done to the pin on the map unless otherwise specified.
