# Hiding Rules

## Valid Hiding Zones

Valid hiding zones include all train stations (S-Bahn, RE, IR, IRE, IC, EC, EXT) within the ZVV zone (**the white area of the map**).
* The **S18**, despite its tram-like appearance, counts as a train station for this game.
* The physical hiding zone extends **500 meters** in every direction around the chosen station.

## Final Hiding Locations

Your final spot must be reachable from the train station **without leaving and reentering your 500m zone**.

### Outside Hiding

* Permitted on all **public property** and free, publicly accessible private property (the public access must be completely clear).
* Hiders **may not lie down**; you must either sit or stand.
* It has to be within 5 meters of a path/road (not counting height difference) that is marked on google maps and within your zone.
* **Forbidden Terrain:** Under water, in the canalisation (sewers), in caves, or on roofs (unless the roof is explicitly accessible via a public path or staircase).
* You must hide in a spot where the seekers would not need to walk more than 10m from a marked path to be able to clearly spot you. This boils down to hiding behind a bush is fine, hiding under one is not.
* It must be a spot where photo of the widest street does not give a null answer.
* You must hide in a spot where the seekers would not need to walk more than 10m from a marked path to be able to clearly spot you. This boils down to hiding behind a bush is fine, hiding under one is not.


### Inside Hiding

Hiding inside buildings is allowed *only* if the space is free to enter, open for the foreseeable duration of the game, does not pose a comfort issue for seekers staying multiple hours, and falls under this strict list:
* **Allowed:** Public buildings (libraries—do not disturb people), malls (public corridors only, not inside individual businesses), and transport stations.
* **Prohibited:** Cafes, restaurants, supermarkets, stores of any kind, public toilets, parking garages (people have been kicked out before) and cinemas.
* *Note:* When inside an allowed building, the Outside Hiding rules do not apply, but the entire route to get there must be publicly accessible during game hours.

> **Out-of-Bounds Emergency:** If you are in a prohibited spot (like a shop or cafe) when the Endgame is triggered, you must immediately walk to the exit of that business and hide right there.

## The Endgame

The Endgame is triggered the moment the seekers **enter the hiders' zone and exit the physical station building/platform area**.

* Once triggered, **hiders can no longer move**.
* If the seekers travel twice the hiding zone radius away from the center of the zone (1000m), the game will continue as if the endgame never happened.
