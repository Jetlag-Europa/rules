## Deck Composition

The following cards from the [**Base Game**](https://jetlag.pages.dev/cards) have been **removed**:

* Curse of the Egg Partner
* Curse of the Endless Tumble
* Curse of the Lemon Phylactery
* 5-minute bonus **x6**

The following **cards from [Expansion Pack Volume 1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/18ORSlF84xPRBIyophPjgvNzt0veiCvUeF69wU1aGhdo/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.u4cyup6b1gyr)** have been **added if Expansion Pack #1 is used**:

* 5% time bonus **x4**
* 10% time bonus **x4**
* 60-minute time bonus **x1** (note: there are four in the deck, but we are only adding one)
* Time trap **x5**
* Discard me **x2**
* Nothing **x5**
* Discard 3, draw 4 **x4**
* Draw 2, expand 2 **x2**
* All curses, **except**:
  * Curse of the Oracle
  * Curse of the Runner
  * Curse of the Okaihau Express
  * Curse of the Seventh Seal
  * Curse of the Strongman
  * Curse of the Post Office

*If we can have at least one expansion pack per team, the expansion pack gets added, otherwise, we're only playing with the home game.*

Please also see [**additional reference rules**](https://rules.jetlagthegame.com/expansion/) for the expansion pack.

## Power-Ups

**Vetos** are now **permanent** and cannot be asked at double cost, as explained on the Layover episode "[Inside the Game Design of Season 16](https://nebula.tv/thelayover/inside-the-game-design-of-season-16-hide-seek-uk)".

**Randomise** does not include the question originally asked. The seekers must use a random number generator to select from the remaining questions in that category. The new question may be abandoned for a null answer.

## Curse Rulings

**🤑 Curse of the Anonymous Benefactor**: A public place is defined as anywhere that is open and accessible to the general public.

🐦‍⬛ **Curse of the Bird Guide**: The bird must be alive and not kept in captivity.

**🧠 Curse of the Empty Mind**: After playing this curse, you may still play any cards in your hand that enable you to draw additional cards (e.g. Discard 1, Draw 2).

**🚪 Curse of the Jammed Door:** If entering a building, a doorway is defined as having full-height doors attached to it (even if they are open) and a ceiling. You do not need to roll dice to enter a different part of the same building. Station shopping centres are separate buildings from stations. You must roll dice before entering an elevator that will take you to a different building. Outdoor shelters with doors are an exception.

**☎️ Curse of the Landline**: "Game-related purposes" does not include asking questions, which is still allowed. A wall outlet is somewhere that takes power from a publicly accessible vehicle or building.

**👸🏻 Curse of the Passenger Princess**: If the seekers are in a team of three, the passenger princess' teammates may split the driver duties between them.

✈️ **Curse of the Planespotter**: An airplane is a powered aircraft with wings. Helicopters, drones, gliders and paper planes do not count.

**🍍 Curse of the Pomologist**: The fruit must not be one that you have brought with you.

**🦢 Curse of the Seabird**: The first sentence should read: "Seekers must photograph a bird within 3 meters of a body of water ***before asking another question***." The bird must be alive, and you may not move it to within 3 m of a body of water.

**🦈 Curse of the Shark**: You may not board any form of transit until this curse has been cleared, even if there is space to walk around the vehicle you want to board. If you are on a form of transit while this curse is played, it will take effect when you alight.

🔫 **Curse of the Sniper**: You do not have to send the photo immediately. However, it must have been taken during the endgame.

**👻 Curse of the Untethered Spirit**: This curse may only be played in the endgame.
