# Seeking & Intel Questions

## Forbidden Tools

When seeking, you are strictly prohibited from using:
* Google Street View
* Reverse image search
* OpenStreetMap complex queries
* Any form of Artificial Intelligence (AI)
* Screenshots from any of the Banned tools
---

## Intel Menu

### Radar Questions
Available distances: `500m`, `1km`, `2km`, `5km`, `10km`, `15km`, `40km`, and `Custom`.

### Thermometer Questions
Available distances: `1km`, `5km`, `10km`, `15km`.

### Tentacle Questions
Queries must check for features within **2 km**:
* Museums
* Libraries
* Movie theatres
* Hospitals

---

## Measuring & Feature Definitions

* Only features on the google maps overlay count for matching and measuring. These features are also imported into Searchlight and you can visualize your asked questions there
* **Searchlight** is the ultimate authority. Measuring must be calculated directly to the map **pin** unless stated otherwise.
