# Mechanics & Clarifications

## Deck Composition & Curses

 we use the standard base game deck with the following overrides:
* **Veto Window:** All active curses can be **vetoed after 60 minutes**. Once vetoed, seekers receive an immediate bonus card pull.
* **Curse Mitigation:** Seekers cannot acquire items or photograph targets to clear a curse *until* they have officially been hit by that specific curse card.

### Specific Curse Clarifications
* **Curse of the Bird Guide:** The target bird must be alive, wild, and not kept in captivity.
* **Curse of the Jammed Door:** A "doorway" requires full-height physical doors attached to it (even if propped open) and a solid ceiling. 
  * You do not need to roll to move between different areas of the same building.
  * Station shopping centers are separate structures from the transit platforms themselves.
  * You must roll dice before entering an elevator if it transfers you to a different building.
  * Outdoor weather shelters featuring doors are exempt from rolls.

## **Power-ups**

**Vetos** are now **permanent** and cannot be asked at double cost, as explained on the Layover episode “[Inside the Game Design of Season 16](https://nebula.tv/thelayover/inside-the-game-design-of-season-16-hide-seek-uk)”.

**Randomise** does not include the question originally asked. The seekers must use a random number generator to select from the remaining questions in that category. The new question may be abandoned for a null answer.

## **Photo Questions**

The **subject** of a photo (excluding “sightline” questions) must be partially or fully located within the hiding zone. 

If the hiders cannot take a photo that fulfills the required criteria (for instance, if no such feature exists, or if they would have to move during the endgame), they may give a **null answer**.

Hiders may start taking photos **as soon as they are in their hiding zone**. They may choose to send these photos at a later time, **except during the endgame**.

This does not apply to **photos taken for curses**, which may be taken and sent at any time.

---

**🏢 Tallest Building Visible from Transit Station / Any Building Visible from Transit Station**

If there are no buildings visible from a particular station entrance, you must choose another entrance to take your photo from. You cannot give a null answer unless there are no buildings visible from any station entrance.

**⛩️ Tallest Structure in Your Current Sightline**  
Although the description of the question refers to a **building**, any permanent immobile man-made **structure** counts. Remember, it’s **from your perspective**, so not necessarily the objectively tallest structure.

**🚸 Trace Nearest Street/Path**  
This must be **north-aligned** and **marked on Google Maps**. The intersection of a path with the edge of the hiding zone counts as an intersection.

**🛣️ Widest Street**  
If asked before the endgame, answer with the **widest street in your hiding zone**. If asked during the endgame, answer with the **widest street in your sightline**. Your photo must be from curb to curb.

## **Matching Questions**

**🚇 Transit Line**

The answer is yes if the transit the seekers are currently riding **will or has** stopped at the hider's station (emphasis added). 

**🔤 Station Name's Length**  
You must answer with ‘**same length’** or ‘**not the same length**.’ All characters count, including spaces and punctuation (e.g. apostrophes, ampersand and parentheses).

You must use the names **as spelled on the game map**. Note that some spellings on the game map may be different from those on Google Maps. **Click on the station icon** to view the correct spelling.

## **Curses**

🐦‍⬛ **Curse of the Bird Guide**: The bird must be alive and not kept in captivity.

**🚪 Curse of the Jammed Door:** If entering a building, a doorway is defined as having full-height doors attached to it (even if they are open) and a ceiling. You do not need to roll dice to enter a different part of the same building. Station shopping centres are separate buildings from stations. You must roll dice before entering an elevator that will take you to a different building. Outdoor shelters with doors are an exception.
