# Transport & Tickets

## Allowed Transport Modes

You are permitted to use **any public transport** covered by the ZVV network. This includes:
* Trains (S-Bahn, Regional, InterCity within the zone, etc.)
* Trams
* Buses
* Ships and Ferries
* Cable cars and Funiculars

You are allowed to leave the game area of Canton Zürich while on public transport as long as you stay within the ZVV network.

## Banned Transport Modes

You may **not** use any private, active, or on-demand transit:
* Bicycles or E-Bikes
* Taxis, Uber, or similar ride-hailing options
* Private cars, scooters, or foot-scooters

## Ticket Recommendations

Every player is strictly responsible for carrying a valid ticket for the transit they board. Recommended options for the day include:
* **ZVV 9-Uhr-Pass** (Valid the whole day on Weekends, 5am - 5am next day)
* **ZVV 24-Hour Ticket** (Covers all your local zones)
* **ZVV Zone Extension** (Anschlussbillett) if you already hold an existing ZVV subscription (like a NetzPass)
* **SBB Day Pass** (Tageskarte) if you are traveling from outside the ZVV network to get to Zürich HB
